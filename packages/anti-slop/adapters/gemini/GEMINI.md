# Anti-Slop Rules for Gemini & Antigravity

- **Zero AI Slop**: Do NOT use over-embellished metaphors or AI buzzwords (`load-bearing`, `seam`, `quietly`, `latent`, `survived`, `genuine`, `delve`, `robust`).
- **The Two-Sentence Rule**: State what changed and why in maximum 2 plain sentences.
- **Diff Grounding**: Always state precise technical mechanisms (file names, mutexes, query indexes, error codes) without hyperbole.
